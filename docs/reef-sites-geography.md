# Reef Sites Geography

## Route framing

The ten main missions now map to a clearer Caribbean-to-Atlantic difficulty route. The game still uses a compact species roster, but each stop now has a geographic identity and a mechanic focus.

## Mission list

1. `Nursery Reef` — Florida Keys, USA
2. `Shallow Flats` — Belize Barrier Reef
3. `Branching Garden` — Cayman Islands
4. `The Ledge` — Bahamas
5. `Coral Nursery` — Turks & Caicos
6. `Overgrowth` — Bonaire
7. `Mid-Water Column` — Curacao
8. `The Deep Wall` — Los Roques, Venezuela
9. `Bleaching Event` — Fernando de Noronha, Brazil
10. `The Reef Summit` — Trinidad & Tobago

## Progression intent

- Early levels stay readable and teach identification, helper fish choice, and the turn loop.
- Mid-route levels introduce stressors and conflicting reef pressures.
- Late-route levels tighten the turn budget and push full-system play with environment, action economy, and harmful species pressure.

## Notes

- These site labels are content framing for the route map and level definitions.
- Species/subject-image assignment can keep evolving independently of the geographic naming layer.
