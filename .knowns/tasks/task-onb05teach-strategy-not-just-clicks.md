# Tutorial must teach strategy, not only button sequence

## Problem

The current tutorial mostly teaches what to click next.
It does not clearly teach why a fish is good or bad for the current reef target.

Observed gap:
- the player learns `tap +`
- the player does not learn a meaningful causal rule such as which fish helps this coral and why that matters for winning

## Why this is urgent

Without one clear causal lesson, the player leaves the tutorial knowing the controls but not the game.

## Required outcome

- Before or during the first feed action, teach one explicit strategic rule
- Example shape: `Blue Chromis helps this coral grow, so adding one pushes the reef toward the target`
- Results screen should reinforce cause/effect, not just show numbers

## Acceptance notes

- A first-time player should be able to answer:
  - what feeding does
  - why they chose that fish
  - how that moved them closer to winning
