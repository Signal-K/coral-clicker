Visual heuristics applied in generator:
- Black coral species (Antipathes, Stichopathes): dark brown/black branch and whip silhouettes.
- Gorgonian/fan corals (Muricea, Swiftia, Thesea): fan/sea-whip branching forms in orange, cream, violet tones.
- Stony/boulder corals (Madracis, Madrepora): denser clustered/knobby structure and brighter tips.
- Sponge: rounded porous silhouette with warm yellow/orange palette.
- Fish choices biased to Caribbean + western Atlantic reef assemblages that co-occur with these corals.
